A note on the title: This page is built around a Dutch play on words. The word hoop means both “hope” and “heap” or “mound.” Molshoop—the Dutch word for “molehill”—literally joins “mole” and “heap.” English separates these meanings, but both run through this story: the mound of earth made by the mole, and the role of hope in facing ecological breakdown.
