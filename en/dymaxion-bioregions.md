# Another way of seeing Earth

While attending the inspiring [R3.0 conference on collapse and bioregioning](https://conference2026.r3-0.org/agenda/),
I had the idea of bringing two world maps together:
the Dymaxion map and One Earth’s bioregions map.

What becomes visible when we view Earth through its ecological
connections?

This remix brings One Earth’s bioregions onto the twenty triangular
faces of the Dymaxion map.

## An exploration, not an exact map

This image was created with AI assistance. Both the bioregion
boundaries and parts of the geography are approximations and may
contain errors. It is intended as a visual exploration, not for
geographical analysis.

It is not an official map or an endorsement by either organization.
A future version can be rebuilt using the original geographical data.
