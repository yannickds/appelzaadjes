# Appelzaadjes

Apple seeds are full of implicit order, full of longing to unfold.
So too, the ideas that visit me are—at best—the starting point of a new story.
Like apple seeds, these ideas need the right context in which to unfold.
My search is about creating that context. And doing so in co-creation.
This website is a means to that end: always in motion, never the final product.
